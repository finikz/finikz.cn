## Illegal French Lesson: Monday Again? I Feel Like Punching the Ground!

[![Illustration](/articles/images/ebe0b6ea6e23f873.gif)](http://p1.s.hjfile.cn/thread/201111/2011110715934836_293_o.gif)

Today, Teacher Fei Ni Kesi is teaching us a French word: *lundi* (Monday). In Mandarin, it sounds a little like “lun di,” meaning “swing your fist down and punch the ground.”

Here’s a way to remember it: it’s Monday, *lundi*—everyone wants to clench a fist and punch the ground.

Just don’t confuse it with *qiang di* in the Chinese idiom *hu tian qiang di* (“crying to heaven and beating the ground”), though the feeling is about the same.

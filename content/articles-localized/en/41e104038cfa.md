# Illegal French Lesson: “The World Is for Everyone”—*Le monde est masculin*

Readers should care about the world and pay attention to “the world,” as people did during the Occupy Wall Street movement a while ago.

[![Illustration](/articles/images/b081422640e55451.jpg)](http://p1.s.hjfile.cn/thread/201111/2011110730130680_265_o.jpg)

This protester even wrote the slogan “All under heaven belongs to the public” in Chinese.

[![Illustration](/articles/images/ed6664059401fc3b.jpg)](http://p1.s.hjfile.cn/thread/201111/2011110730212336_399_o.jpg)

The slogan “All under heaven belongs to the public” became widely known, including overseas, largely because Sun Yat-sen strongly promoted it.

**Meaning:** Is “the world” public property? In French, *le monde* (“the world,” “humanity”) is masculine. The sky (*le ciel*) and heaven (*le paradis*) are masculine, too!

**A note:** People unfamiliar with French or classical Chinese may misunderstand “the world is public” as an expression of machismo.

**Further reading:** The phrase “All under heaven belongs to the public” comes from the *Book of Rites*: “When the Great Way is practiced, all under heaven belongs to the public.”

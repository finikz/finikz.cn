### Chinese covers of French songs · Natural High episode 5

**# Uncover the Covers 02** · 33 minutes

As everyone knows, a lot of Chinese pop songs are covers of English- and Japanese-language songs.

You have probably heard sayings like “Nakajima Miyuki carried half of ’90s Cantopop.”

People say the other half came from Koji Tamaki, or, even earlier, Shinji Tanimura.

Of course, that is an exaggeration. Some Chinese songs were also adapted from European and American music, including French songs.

There are quite a few such songs, but few people know the originals were in French.

This episode is Zhang Zhuren’s solo jam: a collection of Chinese-language covers of French songs.

### How to Pronounce Uniqlo Properly

*2 minutes*

Has Uniqlo taken over your social-media feed too? Sorry to disappoint anyone looking for a funny video: Finikzine is a high-minded, pseudo-literary, fashion-and-tech publication. Ha!

Today we are learning how to pronounce Japanese brand names—properly and elegantly, in both Japanese and Japanese-accented English—whether you are chatting with friends or shopping your way around Japan. Taiwan’s “Don’t Make Trouble” studio invited a Japanese woman to teach us the names of ten Japanese brands. Uniqlo is near the end.
